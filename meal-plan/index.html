Simple Sauces

Make once, keep refrigerated all week.

01

Garlic-Honey Butter

Melted butter + raw honey + pinch salt + minced garlic.

Drizzle on meat
02

Raw Milk Ranch-Style

A2 half & half + melted butter + salt + garlic powder, whisked.

Salad / slaw dressing
03

Maple-Butter

Melted butter + maple syrup + pinch salt.

Steak or fruit side
04

Mustard Vinaigrette

Olive oil + balsamic vinegar + French mustard + garlic + salt + pepper, whisked.

The house salad dressing

The vinaigrette carries real fat (the olive oil), so it's now doing part of the job the meat-sauces used to do alone — that's why the pours below are smaller than before. Mike: 2 tbsp on the salad. Janna: 1.5 tbsp. On the fattiest nights (flap steak, ground beef dinner) it drops to 1 tbsp each, since the meat's already rich enough.

Quick Tips

—

80/20 ground beef only — the fat is the point, don't drain it.

—

Salt is all you need — keeps prep fast and flavor clean.

—

Flap steak always fresh — doesn't reheat as well, worth the extra pan time.

—

Yogurt + banana + honey — grab-and-go for early opener exits; add reheated meat alongside for protein.

—

Wednesday top-up — fresh rice batch + fresh flap steak keeps everything fresh through the week.

—

Heart & Soil supplements cover organ nutrition — no liver cooking needed.

!

Romaine/cabbage salad is technically a "minimize" item under the strict animal-based hierarchy (plant defense compounds), but it's well-tolerated and kept in by preference.

!

French mustard is technically a seed too, same category as the cabbage above — a teaspoon or two whisked into a batch of dressing is a small enough amount that it's not worth stressing over.
